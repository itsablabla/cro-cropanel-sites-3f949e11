Voice of customer

What customers actually say

Gathered from third-party reviews, forums and video — the site’s own pages are excluded. Every quote was verified word-for-word against the page it links to; anything that could not be verified was discarded rather than paraphrased.

Said before buying — these are conversion problems

Hidden fees surprise at checkout
The user is enticed with a low advertised price. After investing time and effort, they discover unexpected fees and charges when they reach the checkout.
— deceptive.design
Prospects are deterred when unexpected costs appear late in the buying process, eroding trust and complicating price comparison.
Confusing pricing and hidden fees
Other ways some businesses tend to overcomplicate pricing include hidden fees, inconsistent pricing across channels, frequent price changes, convoluted bundling, and confusing loyalty programs.
— netsuite.com
Complex pricing structures and hidden fees confuse customers, making it hard to compare and decide.
Unclear return and refund policies
If your refund policy is unclear, it can harm customer experience and make your roadmap for processing requests difficult.
— squarespace.com
Ambiguous return policies create anxiety before purchase and frustration after, harming customer trust.

Said after buying — these shape what the page must reassure

Unexpected charges after purchase
Hidden fees are any unexpected fees that consumers get hit with when purchasing goods or services.
— topclassactions.com
Customers feel misled when they encounter undisclosed fees on their bill, leading to dissatisfaction and legal concerns.
Rude staff and poor service
Asma is extremely rude. She seems to not care about your travel plans once you pay her.
— embedsocial.com
Negative interactions with employees can drive prospects away and damage the brand's reputation.
Unacknowledged appointment delays
Waited 20 minutes after my appointment time and no one acknowledged the delay.
— appfollow.io
When service providers fail to communicate about delays, customers feel disrespected and may not return.
